# Current creative guardrail

The latest website handoff brief is the source of truth. Preserve Omnia's welcome-screen atmosphere, frost/lavender/violet/mint palette, segmented mark, and local Manrope, while rethinking the presentation around them.

Omnia is a global, consulting-led B2B technology company. A client brings an ambition, operational problem, or existing technology; Omnia's team determines how connected systems, custom software, automation and AI could help. The public site should communicate outcomes and possibility—not read like an agency brochure, chatbot platform, dashboard builder, fixed-feature SaaS, or investor architecture deck.

Give each section a distinct narrative purpose. Show how combining systems can create a capability; show global, regional and custom breadth without claiming integrations are live; combine consulting and tailored experience into one human promise; keep Omnia OS optional and clearly in development. Do not invent features, customers, partnerships, releases, traction, or proven scalability. Initial validation markets do not limit the global ambition.

The prelaunch ending introduces future launch updates and conversations with clients, partners, and investors. Collection is deferred at the owner's request. Show opening-soon invitations until a real destination is configured.
