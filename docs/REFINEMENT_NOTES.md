# Possibility-led refinement

## Plan

Colors: frost #F2F0FA, violet #5B45D6, lavender #C9C2F2, mint #69E3C0, with the uploaded deep-violet section retained. Type: local Manrope, clear display hierarchy and readable short paragraphs. Layout: welcome → promise and convergence → imagined scenes → ecosystem → client brief and outcome → quiet foundation with optional depth → prelaunch invitations.

Spend the visual emphasis on the logo translating a client's brief into an experience. Preserve the uploaded interactions. Reduce machinery language, feature-list framing, and defensive category comparisons. Use examples to open possibilities rather than limit the business to a menu. Defer technical detail through a real native disclosure.

## Review against the brief

A conventional architecture-led page would overemphasize the company's proposed business model. The refinement instead makes the business outcome primary and leaves the architecture accessible on request. The third studio brief now explores an entirely new customer capability, rather than another report or dashboard. Existing integration targets remain illustrative. No extra roadmap, products, or performance claims were added.

## Implementation

- Shortened hero and introductory story; client imagination is the invitation.
- Four uploaded scenes and local logos retained.
- Three selectable briefs retain the logo assembly gesture; replay subsequently removed.
- Defensive “not another” strip replaced by an open invitation.
- Simple foundation visual precedes a closed architecture disclosure.
- Form collection deferred per the owner's explicit instruction; removed unconfigured submission handling and retired the old confirmation page.

## Entry and control cleanup

The welcome fade previously revealed the hero before starting a second entrance animation at gate removal. Removed that second entrance. Initialize the destination and ambient scenes behind the opaque gate, keep scrolling stopped through the transition, and reserve scrollbar space so unlocking the page does not shift the layout.

Removed replay and ecosystem filters that merely dimmed groups already present. Replaced OS selector buttons with four short readable entries behind the existing disclosure. Kept only tabs that reveal different stories or client briefs, with an early return when the selected tab is clicked again.

## Disclosure polish

Added reusable progressive enhancement for all details elements. Opening and closing use measured content height with reversible Web Animations, a coordinated content fade/translation, and icon rotation. Closing marks the content inert immediately and keeps the native details open only until the height transition completes. Motion preferences are respected and native keyboard/no-JavaScript behavior remains available.
