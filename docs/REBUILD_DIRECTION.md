# Omnia: a clearer, explorable introduction

## Owner decisions

Omnia consults inside businesses. Its engineers create custom applications, dashboards, integrations and other software solutions. Ongoing support and improvements form a monthly relationship. Applications and dashboards should be tangible public outcomes. The experience should be explorable, surprising, animated, and retain the violet/lavender/mint identity and logo-focused welcome. Detailed art direction is delegated to the developer.

## Research actually reviewed

Live browsing to Linear, Stripe, Apple, Instrument and other reference sites was blocked by the environment. No claim is made to have visually audited those live sites. Public official source and guidance were accessible:

- Anthropic frontend design skill: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md — deliberate visual identity, clear copy, purposeful interaction; downloaded and read. It matches the available project skill.
- Astro official hero and introduction: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/withastro/astro.build/main/src/pages/_components/landing-page/Hero.astro and Intro.astro — explicit category and outcome before demonstrations. Study the information hierarchy, not its gradient styling.
- PostHog official homepage source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/posthog/posthog.com/master/src/components/Home/Test/index.tsx — direct product demonstration alongside readable explanation; separate meaningful actions. Its desktop metaphor and product-specific content are not copied.
- IBM Carbon motion overview: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/carbon-design-system/carbon-website/main/src/pages/elements/motion/overview.mdx
- IBM Carbon choreography: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/carbon-design-system/carbon-website/main/src/pages/elements/motion/choreography.mdx — continuity, spatial relationships, consistent motion for consistent actions, expressive movement concentrated in meaningful moments. These sources are from the archived public Carbon website repository, not a review of the current site.

No psychological study or conversion claim is inferred from those references.

## Design plan

Palette: frost #F2F0FA, violet #5B45D6, lavender #C9C2F2, mint #69E3C0, ink #261E50. Manrope stays as the local typeface; display is large, tightly spaced and readable, body copy has a generous line height. No monospace pseudo-technical labeling.

Layout: an explicit opening paired with a dimensional custom application; a composed set of software examples; one immersive connected product illustration; integration reach; a tangible engineering relationship; animated questions; a real inquiry invitation.

[ Clear offer | Layered custom software ]
[ Different software forms, composed at different scales ]
[ One business ambition -> explore client / learner / delivery ]
[ Familiar platforms + regional + proprietary ]
[ Consult -> engineer -> support ]
[ Questions | Inquiry ]

The main copy is left aligned. The experience stage has stable navigation and a shared application surface. Panels change as perspectives, not as a four-chapter text slideshow. The learner's choice creates a review item in the team's view. Opening the build explanation reveals the existing tools plus the software Omnia would engineer. All data is fictional and every scene is illustrative.

The illustration: a training company wants to sell learning shaped around a client's real workplace situations. Omnia could build its branded client experience, practice sessions, custom delivery workspace and the connections behind them. This is a new service the business can offer, not just faster administration. It is one example of custom software, not an Omnia product or shipped client project.

## Critique before implementation

A floating brand symbol, decorative connection graph, generic feature-card grid or repeated oversized slogan could fit almost any technology company. Those treatments are rejected here. Actual app surfaces carry the visuals. The opening uses the words consulting, engineering, custom applications, dashboards and integrations. The relationship explicitly includes monthly support. Every interaction has a visible consequence, keyboard operation and a reduced-motion form. No user data leaves the illustration.
