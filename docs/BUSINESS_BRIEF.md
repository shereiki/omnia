# Current business context

Omnia is a premium, technology-first company building Omnia OS, a persistent, extensible, deeply customizable, AI-native environment integrated with existing business technology.

Three public product concepts govern the site: Omnia OS provides the foundation; Omnia Modules add or extend capabilities; Solutions combine the foundation, Modules, native intelligence, configuration and custom engineering around an organization's needs. Modules are a public marketing concept. Their four internal engineering categories remain Connectors, Extensions, Automations and Custom Services.

AI and the agent runtime are native to Omnia OS. Agents are not a fifth Module type, and the OS is not limited to AI. Internal and customer-facing agents can be shaped around different responsibilities, permitted knowledge, authorized capabilities, behavior, autonomy and oversight. Customer-facing agents must not imply access to restricted internal or other customers' information.
