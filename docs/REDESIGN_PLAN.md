# Current plan

The redesign implemented in October 2026 follows `WEBSITE_VISION.md`, `ARCHITECTURE_ADDITION.md`, and the latest handoff brief. See `DESIGN_NOTES.md` for presentation and behavior.

## What changed and why

The previous site read as a collection of similar explanations (hero, possibilities, consulting steps, and a tailored-fit diagram all restating “tell us what you want; we connect your tools”), with tentative qualifiers on every card, external favicon dependencies for regional marks, and a dead-end prelaunch section. The redesign keeps the welcome screen, palette, and segmented mark, and rebuilds the narrative:

- Each section now carries one distinct idea, in order: statement → capability scenes → ecosystem reach → transformation (consulting + tailored fit, unified) → the Omnia OS foundation → two honest pathways to engage.
- The possibilities theatre shows multi-system chain reactions and the new capability each creates, instead of two narrow demos that read as a chatbot and a simple automation.
- Consulting and the tailored-experience interaction are merged into one interactive brief-to-reality studio, eliminating the overlap.
- Qualifying language is consolidated into single, confident notes per section.
- Regional identity marks are locally hosted official assets; no external requests.
- The ending is functional: a waitlist and a direct conversation route, both handled by Netlify Forms with truthful feedback in every state.

## Preserved

The welcome experience, brand palette, segmented Omnia symbol, Manrope, rounded tactile controls, autonomous logo motion, the outward launch gesture, Lenis smooth scrolling, viewport/reduced-motion governance, and the JavaScript-free readable fallback.
