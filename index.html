The systems you rely on stay in place.

Most businesses run on a stack of tools that don't talk to each other. Omnia OS sits underneath them as one shared foundation.

Then we connect what stays, so records, requests and approvals move between your tools on their own. When you're ready to grow, new capabilities plug into the same foundation instead of becoming one more tool to manage.

FAQ

Questions.

What does Omnia do?+

Omnia connects the software your business already uses into one system. We start by cutting duplicate tools and manual handoffs, then add the capabilities you're missing through four kinds of Modules: Connectors, Extensions, Automations and Custom Services. Our team implements it and keeps it running.

Do we have to replace our existing software?+

No. We start with what you already rely on, keep what works, connect it, and only add software where something is missing.

How is this different from buying another app?+

Another app means another login, another bill and another place for data to drift. Omnia works underneath the tools you keep, so new capabilities share the same records and permissions instead of starting from scratch.

What happens after implementation?+

We stay involved to manage, support and improve what we built, on terms agreed around your business.
